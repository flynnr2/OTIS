## Interim containment

Catch ordinary diagnostic exceptions inside the service loop, preserving traceback
and authority inhibition. Both normal and fallback hold paths use the same
admitted ownership-lease service. Expired evidence deadlines remain expired but
do not disable an independent bounded lease renewal during review. Unresolved
normal command writes still prevent further normal commands; no ambiguous command
is replayed and real transport obstruction may still cause firmware fail-static.

Regressions check every self-method call across the supervisor inheritance chain,
the actual discontinuity branch, fallback lease service and expired evidence
versus unresolved-write behavior. The production PTY rehearsal raises an actual
AttributeError while the second response is pending, requires exact response
completion through the hold, then continued leases/capture, no new authority,
independent abort, closure, analysis and sealing. This is stronger than directly
injecting a preconstructed hold, but does not recreate a physical post-origin
D14 epoch change or prove that such a transaction can recover scientifically.

## Finite replacement boundary

Replace the duplicated host control lifecycle, retaining working serial capture,
protocol and immutable evidence tools. One lifecycle service should own lease
renewal, explicit abort and closure. Scientific/transaction consumers return
bounded decisions or holds; their exceptions cannot displace ownership service.
Keep evidence deadlines, ownership deadlines and firmware counter-domain expiry
separate. Preserve all pending transaction identities and never infer approval
from elapsed time. Use the retained failures and the strengthened operational
rehearsal as the acceptance contract for that replacement.

Resolve post-origin D14 loss policy explicitly before another long run: the
present campaign cannot join acceptance epochs or automatically clear such a
hold. A successful containment test does not make this a successful steering
recovery policy. The current firmware remains unchanged.

Chat reporting is a distinct pre-entry gate. Verify delivery of material events,
steering opportunities, faults and the agreed hourly status cadence; local logs
or a running observer alone do not prove notification delivery. A budget-dependent
chat service cannot promise immediate delivery while no model capacity exists;
state that limitation or verify an independent delivery mechanism. No automation
was created or changed by this development work.
